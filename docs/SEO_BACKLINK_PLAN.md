## 2. On-page work already implemented

- Keyword-led home title and description, plus a substantial **“A bridal boutique in Kurnool, built around the fitting”** section with links to services, localities and journal guides.
- Keyword-rich static metadata for `/`, `/about`, `/services`, `/products`, `/gallery`, `/portfolio`, `/blogs`, `/contact`, `/request-service` and `/kurnool`.
- Per-product and per-article SEO title/description normalised to display lengths.
- Local business schema: full address, geo coordinates, map, phone, email and opening hours.
- Product, Article, Service, CreativeWork, FAQPage, ItemList and ImageGallery schema.
- 160-URL sitemap, prerendered HTML for every indexable route, canonical tags and Open Graph/Twitter cards.
- Internal links from the home page to bridal, traditional, tailoring, styling, products, gallery, journal guides and each locality page.
- Blog posts expanded to at least ~150 words with unique studio copy.
- 10 keyword-focused FAQs on the home page, mirrored in FAQPage schema.

## 3. Backlink targets

Backlinks must be earned or submitted as a real business. Do not buy links,
use PBNs or mass-post the same text to directories.

### Tier 1 — must-have local citations

| Site | What to submit | Link target |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Verified address, phone, hours, 10+ photos, services, posts | `https://kadambastudio.com/` |
| Justdial | Business listing, category “Boutique”, hours, photos | `/` and `/contact` |
| Sulekha | Boutique / tailoring listing | `/services` |
| IndiaMART / TradeIndia | Tailoring / boutique service listing if applicable | `/services/custom-tailoring` |
| Bing Places | Mirror of Google Business Profile | `/` |
| Apple Business Connect | Local listing | `/` |
| Kurnool local directories / chamber of commerce | Local business citation | `/kurnool` |

### Tier 3 — editorial links

Pitch one original story at a time, with a real image and a specific angle:

1. **“What a bridal fitting actually involves in a small Indian atelier”** — to wedding blogs and fashion magazines.
2. **“The case for the second fitting”** — to bridal planning sites.
3. **“How Kurnool's summer heat changes a wedding wardrobe”** — to regional lifestyle publications.
4. **“Inside a 15-year-old Kurnool atelier”** — to local news / city magazines.
5. **“Maggam work: what it costs and why”** — to craft and textile blogs.
